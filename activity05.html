Activity 5: Simple Car

For this project, we created simple Lego cars using our SPIKE Prime kits. I worked on this car in conjunction with Parviz S. We decided to divide and conqure, so Parviz constructed the actual car while I wrote the code using the EN1 "coderobots" page making use of AI-powered code generation. We tried to keep the car as simple as possible, while also trying to make it look a bit unique (we added a spoiler!).
